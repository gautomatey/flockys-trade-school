How the bag thinks

Position size

Shares = dollar risk ÷ |entry − stop|. Dollar risk = bag × risk %. We floor shares to a whole number. If shares × entry is bigger than cash left, we shrink shares until the ticket fits the bag.

Why 1–2%

A $500 bag risking 2% loses $10 if stopped. Ten losses in a row = $100 (20%). Risking 10% needs only ten losers to empty the bag. Small accounts die from fat tickets, not from missing a runner.

Real-tape examples

Tesla, Apple, SPY, QQQ, NVIDIA use the same last print as Chart House. A $500 bag cannot buy 10 TSLA at ~$357. It can buy 1 share, or none if the stop is so tight that the math still overspends. That is the lesson.

R multiple

R = |target − entry| ÷ |entry − stop|. Under 1R the juice is rarely worth the squeeze. 2R means the win pays for two equal losses.

The four rooms

When the tape is awake

Premarket

4:00 AM–9:30 AM ET. News lands here. Spreads are wider. A gap at the open often starts from the last premarket print.

Regular hours

9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET. Most volume. This is the school day your 1D candle is built from if RTH is on.

After close

4:00 PM–8:00 PM ET. Earnings often print here. The 1D candle on many apps is already closed; this is extra.

Overnight

8:00 PM–4:00 AM ET. Few players. Your TradingView screenshot marked Overnight as its own line. Do not size a day-trade stop off a lonely overnight wick unless you accept the gap.
